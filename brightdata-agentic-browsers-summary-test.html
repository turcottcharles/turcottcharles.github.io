Agentic Browsers: Article Summary

Bright Data describes agentic browsers as AI-driven tools that can interpret context, adapt to changing websites, and complete multi-step web tasks, unlike brittle traditional scripts. Its 2026 comparison says there is no universal winner: Bright Data targets enterprise-scale automation and built-in anti-bot handling; Browser Use and Vercel Agent Browser favor developers and open-source flexibility; Perplexity Comet and ChatGPT Atlas suit conversational consumer workflows; and other products specialize in research, infrastructure, privacy, or no-code automation.

The article recommends choosing by use case, autonomy, technical skill, CAPTCHA and anti-detection needs, concurrency, privacy/compliance, framework support, and total cost. Its practical advice is to define the task first, test a small pilot, measure success and cost, then scale only after reliability and security are proven.
