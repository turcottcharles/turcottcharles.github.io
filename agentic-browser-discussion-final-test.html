ChatGPT’s opinion was that agents should resolve ambiguity while deterministic code enforces authority. A safe hybrid should give agents least-privilege, task-scoped access; treat webpage content as untrusted evidence; validate proposed actions through a deterministic policy gateway; require independent or human approval for consequential actions; verify preconditions and outcomes mechanically; fail closed; and keep complete, replayable audit logs. In short: use an agentic planner and navigator, followed by a policy firewall, narrow executor, and deterministic verifier.
